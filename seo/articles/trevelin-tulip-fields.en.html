The Trevelin tulip fields are an open-air tulip farm in the Andes of Chubut, in Argentine Patagonia, that can only be visited while it blooms in spring. For 2026, the estimated season runs from October 7 to November 7, and admission is ARS 45,000 per person: you pay on site, with any payment method, and no booking is needed.

What it is and where it is

The fields belong to Tulipanes Patagonia and sit on National Route 259, km 50, in Trevelin, Chubut Province. You walk between rows of tulips in different colors, with the Andes in the background.

Trevelin is a town in the 16 de Octubre Valley founded by Welsh settlers; its name means "mill town" in Welsh. Tulips are not native to Patagonia: what you visit is a cultivated farm, not a wild bloom.

When the tulips bloom: 2026 season

The estimated 2026 season runs from October 7 to November 7. These dates are the farm's own estimate, so it's worth confirming them before you travel.

The fields bloom in stages: not every section flowers at the same time, and colors and varieties change from week to week. That's why the landscape at the start of the season is different from the one at the end.

Admission and payment

  • Price: ARS 45,000 per person (2026 season).
  • Payment: on site, with any payment method.
  • Booking: not required.

Information taken from the official Tulipanes Patagonia admission page (in Spanish) on October 22, 2026. Prices may change: check before you go.

How to get there

The fields are on National Route 259, at km 50, in Trevelin. Trevelin is in northwestern Chubut, near Esquel, which has an airport (EQS). If you have questions about how to get there, the farm answers through its official channels.
